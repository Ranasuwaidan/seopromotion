SEO Promotion Plan

Website Goal:
Improve website visibility on search engines and attract more visitors.

Target Audience:
People searching online for products and services.

Keywords:
Best products, affordable products, quality products.

SEO Strategies:

Use relevant keywords in page titles and descriptions.

Add meta tags to the HTML page.

Create mobile-friendly website content.

Add social media icons and links.

Share website content on social media.

Success Measurement:
Monitor website traffic, search rankings, and visitor engagement.
